How to become an Account Owner

Starkworth manages your AI freelance platform account professionally — so you earn without doing the work yourself. Here is exactly how it works:

01

Reach out to us

Contact Starkworth through our contact form or directly. We will explain everything about how we manage AI freelance platform accounts and answer all your questions.

02

Get a full explanation

We walk you through exactly how the process works, how your earnings are shared and how your account is kept safe. No pressure — just clear honest information.

03

Verify your identity

All you need is a valid ID card for verification. Starkworth will guide you through the full account opening process — we handle everything else.

04

Complete live screening

You will need a laptop for a short live screening session on the platform. This is a standard requirement by the AI freelance platforms — Starkworth will prepare you fully.

05

Sign the agreement

Once you are comfortable and ready you sign our profit sharing agreement. Starkworth manages your account and you receive your earnings every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST).

06

Start earning

Starkworth's team manages your account across AI-based freelance platforms, and your weekly payment arrives every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST).

You do not need any prior experience with AI freelance platforms. Starkworth handles everything from account setup to daily management.
