About StarkWorth

We believe profit sharing should be simple, transparent and fair for everyone involved.

Who we are

Built on trust, driven by fairness

Starkworth manages AI data-annotation accounts on behalf of Account Owners, and pays Annotators for the work they complete on those accounts. Every relationship is built on a signed, plain-English agreement that spells out exactly how earnings are split.

Whether you are an Account Owner or an Annotator (AI job specialist), Account Owners review and sign a clear agreement outlining exactly how profits are split. Annotators register and are assigned to projects under the same transparent framework. No surprises. No disputes. Just honest business.

Our process

How StarkWorth works

Clear agreements

Every Account Owner signs a detailed profit-sharing agreement before any work begins. The terms are written in plain English — no confusing legal jargon.

Secure storage

All signed agreements and Annotator registrations are stored securely in our system. Both parties receive confirmation by email.

Team management

Annotators register directly on the platform, making it easy to manage your entire team in one place with full visibility.

Profit distribution

When profits are distributed, everyone already knows the terms. Payments are made according to the signed agreement — no renegotiation needed.
