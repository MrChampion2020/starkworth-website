Referral rules

Standard affiliate terms for every approved affiliate:

  • Earn a standard 5% commission on the eligible weekly earnings of each successful direct referral.
  • A referral is eligible only after the referred person completes onboarding successfully.
  • The referred account must remain actively managed by Starkworth and continue earning from active tasks.
  • Commissions are calculated on verified eligible earnings, not on registrations alone.
  • Cancelled, suspended, inactive, or non-earning accounts do not generate commission for that period.
  • Commission, payout, and withdrawal records are shown in the affiliate dashboard and are subject to review.
