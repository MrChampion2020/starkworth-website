STARKWORTH
Profit Sharing Agreement
This is a legally binding agreement. Please read every section carefully. By signing below you confirm you have read, understood and agreed to all terms stated in this document.

1. Parties to this Agreement

This Profit Sharing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of signing below ("Effective Date") between:

Service Provider Starkworth LLC, a Limited Liability Company registered in Alabama, USA ("Starkworth," "we," "us") AI Training & Account Management
and
Account Owner The account holder named and signing below ("Account Owner," "you") Whose account is actively managed by Starkworth

Starkworth and the Account Owner may each be referred to individually as a "Party" and together as the "Parties."

2. Definitions

  • "Account" means the specific AI data-annotation account the Account Owner has verified and enrolled under this Agreement.
  • "Platform" means the third-party AI data-annotation service on which the Account operates.
  • "Gross Earnings" means the total, unadjusted earnings generated by the Account for a given weekly period.
  • "Net Earnings" means Gross Earnings after deduction of operating and management costs, as calculated by Starkworth and applied to determine the Account Owner Share.
  • "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday observed in Alabama, USA.
  • "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Section 12.

3. Nature of Services

Starkworth provides professional AI training and management services for AI freelancing-based platforms on behalf of the Account Owner. Starkworth manages the day-to-day operations of the Account, including task completion, quality assurance, and performance optimisation, with the aim of generating consistent earnings from the Account.

The Account Owner grants Starkworth the authority to operate and manage the Account for the duration that active tasks remain available on the Account and this Agreement remains in effect. The Account Owner represents that they have the legal right to grant this authority and that doing so does not, to their knowledge, breach any other agreement they are party to.

4. Profit Sharing Terms

Account Owner Share Net Earnings distributed after all operating and management costs are satisfied.
Starkworth Share Starkworth retains the remaining earnings, covering the scale of operations, staffing, and management of the Account.
Payment Schedule Every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST). Payments falling due are processed within this window on each scheduled payment day.
Payment Basis Payment is calculated on the Gross Earnings of the Account for that week, less operating and management costs.

The exact percentage split applicable to the Account Owner Share is confirmed with you individually before signing and is not a fixed, publicly advertised rate. Starkworth will provide a weekly earnings summary upon reasonable request so the Account Owner can review how their payout was calculated.

5. Agreement Duration and Termination

This Agreement remains active and binding for as long as there are active tasks available on the Account, unless ended earlier under this section. This Agreement does not have a fixed end date.

This Agreement will end upon the earliest of the following:

  • The Account becomes permanently inactive or is closed
  • There are no active or pending tasks on the Account for a continuous period of 30 days
  • Either Party provides written notice of termination to the other, with a minimum of 7 days' notice
  • Starkworth determines, in its reasonable professional judgement, that continued management is no longer viable
  • Either Party materially breaches this Agreement and, where curable, fails to cure that breach within 10 Business Days of written notice

Upon termination, Starkworth will calculate and pay any Net Earnings owed to the Account Owner up to the effective date of termination, on the next scheduled payment date. Sections 4 (as to earnings already accrued), 12 (Confidentiality), 13 (Data Protection), 15 (Limitation of Liability), and 19 (Governing Law) survive termination of this Agreement.

6. Account Owner Representations and Warranties

The Account Owner represents and warrants that:

  • They are at least 18 years old and have full legal capacity to enter into this Agreement
  • All information provided during verification, screening, and signing is true, accurate, and complete
  • They are the legitimate holder of the Account and have the right to authorize Starkworth to manage it
  • Entering into this Agreement does not violate any other contract, agreement, or legal obligation they are subject to

7. Account Owner Responsibilities

By entering into this Agreement the Account Owner agrees to:

  • Provide Starkworth with valid and secure access to their Account
  • Not interfere with or independently operate the Account during the management period without prior written consent from Starkworth
  • Notify Starkworth immediately of any changes to Account credentials or Account status
  • Not share Account access with any third party outside of Starkworth
  • Provide accurate personal and payment details for the purpose of receiving weekly payments, and keep them up to date

8. Starkworth Responsibilities

Starkworth agrees to:

  • Manage the Account professionally and in good faith at all times
  • Make every reasonable effort to maximise earnings from the Account
  • Pay the Account Owner Share every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST) without undue delay
  • Provide the Account Owner with a weekly earnings summary upon reasonable request
  • Keep the Account Owner's credentials and personal information strictly confidential, consistent with Sections 12 and 13
  • Notify the Account Owner promptly of any issues materially affecting the Account or expected earnings

9. Payment Method and Details

Weekly payments will be made via the payment method agreed between both Parties at the time of onboarding. Full payment details are collected only after this Agreement is signed and approved, inside an authenticated dashboard, never on a public form. The Account Owner is responsible for ensuring their payment details are accurate and up to date. Starkworth accepts no liability for failed or misdirected payments resulting from incorrect payment details provided by the Account Owner.

If a Wednesday payment is missed due to a bank holiday, banking-system delay, or technical issue outside Starkworth's reasonable control, Starkworth will make the payment on the next available Business Day.

10. Taxes

Each Party is solely responsible for determining and satisfying their own tax obligations, including income tax, self-employment tax, or any equivalent obligation under the law applicable to them, arising from amounts paid or received under this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement constitutes tax advice, and Starkworth does not withhold taxes on the Account Owner's behalf unless required to do so by applicable law.

11. Independent Relationship

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the Parties, except to the limited extent that the Account Owner authorizes Starkworth to manage the Account as described in Section 3. Each Party acts as an independent party in relation to the other for all other purposes, including tax and benefits purposes.

12. Confidentiality

Both Parties agree to keep all details of this Agreement, including earnings figures, Account details, and operational methods ("Confidential Information"), strictly confidential. Neither Party shall disclose the terms of this Agreement to any third party without the written consent of the other Party, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process, or to that Party's professional advisors under a duty of confidentiality.

13. Data Protection

Each Party will handle any personal data received from the other in connection with this Agreement in accordance with applicable data protection law and Starkworth's Privacy Policy, which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

14. Intellectual Property

Each Party retains ownership of its own pre-existing intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Account itself, which remains the Account Owner's, or of any Platform-specific intellectual property, which remains governed by that Platform's own terms.

15. Limitation of Liability

Starkworth shall not be held liable for:

  • Reduced earnings due to Platform technical changes outside of Starkworth's control
  • Account suspension or termination by the Platform as a result of Platform policy violations
  • Periods of low or no task availability on the Platform
  • Any indirect, incidental, or consequential losses arising from Account management

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, each Party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement, other than for a Party's confidentiality or data-protection obligations, shall not exceed the total Account Owner Share paid or payable in the three months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

16. Indemnification

Each Party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other Party from claims, losses, or expenses, including reasonable legal fees, arising from that Party's material breach of this Agreement, negligence, or violation of applicable law.

17. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement where such delay or failure results from causes beyond that Party's reasonable control, including Platform outages, internet or banking infrastructure failures, or governmental action.

18. Amendment and Assignment

This Agreement may only be amended by written agreement signed by both Parties. The Account Owner may not assign this Agreement without Starkworth's prior written consent. Starkworth may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, provided the assignee agrees to honour its terms.

19. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Alabama, USA, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through good-faith negotiation between both Parties. If unresolved within 30 days, the dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Alabama, USA, except where applicable consumer-protection law in the Account Owner's country of residence provides otherwise.

20. General Provisions

This Agreement, together with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes any prior discussions or agreements on that subject. If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force, and the unenforceable provision will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable. A Party's failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of that provision. Notices under this Agreement should be sent to contact@starkworth.org or to the Account Owner's email address on file.
