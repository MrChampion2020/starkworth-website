General

Starkworth manages AI data-annotation accounts on behalf of Account Owners under a signed profit-sharing agreement, while Annotators do the work and are paid weekly for it.

An Annotator is a person who registers with Starkworth, gets approved, and performs AI data-annotation tasks — labeling images, text, audio, or video according to a platform's guidelines — on a Starkworth-managed account. Annotators don't own or manage the account itself; that's the Account Owner's role. Instead, Annotators are paid weekly based on their task performance and input, every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST), as set out in their registration agreement.

Referral Program

An ongoing 5% override on your referral's weekly earnings, for as long as their account or registration stays active. It's paid on top of your own payout — it doesn't reduce what the person you referred earns. Both Account Owners and Annotators can refer, and there's no cap on how many people you refer or how long the override lasts. Starkworth staff members referring someone earn 10% instead of the standard 5% — see below.
