What Does 'Account Management as a Service' Mean?

A plain-English explanation of the Account Management as a Service (AMaaS) model that describes how Starkworth operates.

Introduction

"Account Management as a Service" (AMaaS) describes a business model where a company manages a client's account on their behalf under a defined agreement, rather than the client running it themselves or being directly employed to do so. Starkworth's FAQ disclaimer explicitly frames its operations this way.

For Account Owners, this means Starkworth handles task completion, quality assurance, and performance optimisation on your account, while you retain ownership and receive your share of Net Earnings.

Key Points

  • AMaaS means a third party manages your account's operations under a signed agreement, not that they own it.
  • It's standard practice for this kind of model to include disclaimers about platform volatility, since the manager doesn't control the underlying platform.
  • You remain the Account Owner and legal holder of the account throughout.
  • The agreement defines exactly what Starkworth will and won't be responsible for.

Why the Distinction Matters

Calling this AMaaS rather than employment or partnership is precise: Section 11 of the agreement states this is an independent relationship, and Starkworth's FAQ disclaimer notes such statements are standard practice for AMaaS models specifically to address liability around third-party platform volatility.
