Bank Transfer vs PayPal: Which Payment Method Should You Choose?

A comparison of bank transfer and PayPal for Starkworth payouts, to help you choose the right preferred payment method at registration.

Introduction

Starkworth supports bank transfer, PayPal, and other payment methods considered case by case, and you select your preference during registration. Neither option requires you to submit actual account numbers, routing details, or your PayPal email on the public registration form — you only indicate your preferred method at that stage.

Who This Applies To

Both Account Owners and Annotators choose a preferred payment method during onboarding.

Why It Matters

Choosing the right method for your situation up front avoids unnecessary delays later — the right choice depends on your country, how quickly you want access to funds, and what you already use for other income.

What's Involved

  • Bank transfer: funds land directly in a local account without an intermediary platform
  • PayPal: often faster access, useful if you're already using it for other freelance income
  • Other methods: considered case by case — raise specifics with Support

Step-by-Step Process

You select your preferred method category during registration or onboarding. Full payment details — account numbers, PayPal email, or wallet address — are collected afterward, once your application is approved, inside your authenticated dashboard, never on the public form.

What You Need to Get Started

  • A decision on which method suits your country and situation
  • Full payment details ready to enter once approved (not before)

Typical Timeline

Your preferred method is selected at registration; full details are added post-approval, and from then on payouts follow the standard weekly Wednesday schedule regardless of which method you chose.

What Determines Your Terms

Starkworth doesn't use fixed public pricing tiers — every Account Owner and Annotator works under an individually signed agreement. The figures below explain what shapes those terms, not a price list.

Neither method carries a Starkworth-side fee structure publicized here — any processing time or cost differences come from your bank or PayPal itself, which varies by country.

Ongoing Support

If you're unsure which method fits your situation, Support can walk through the tradeoffs for your specific country before you register.
