How to Become a Starkworth Account Owner: Step-by-Step Guide

The complete six-step process for becoming a Starkworth Account Owner, from initial contact through signing your profit-sharing agreement.

Introduction

Becoming an Account Owner follows six steps, and none of them are binding until you sign. The process is designed so you understand exactly what you're agreeing to before verification even begins — nobody is asked to hand over identity documents before they've had the model fully explained.

Who This Applies To

Anyone considering becoming an Account Owner, from complete beginners with no AI freelance platform experience to people who've researched the space already.

Why It Matters

Following the steps in order — and not skipping the explanation step — is what makes the rest of the process fast and low-friction. Most delays happen when someone rushes into verification without having their questions answered first.

What's Involved

  • Initial contact and no-pressure explanation of the model
  • Identity verification with a valid government ID
  • A short live screening session on a laptop
  • Reviewing and signing the profit-sharing agreement
  • Ongoing account management once you're live

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Reach out through the Contact page — Starkworth explains the process and answers questions with no pressure.
  2. Get a full explanation of exactly how earnings are shared and how your account is kept safe.
  3. Verify your identity with a valid ID card.
  4. Complete a live screening session on a laptop — a standard platform requirement.
  5. Sign the agreement, which sets your exact split and payment schedule.
  6. Start earning — your account goes live and Starkworth's team manages it going forward.

What You Need to Get Started

  • A valid, unexpired government-issued ID
  • A laptop with a working camera and microphone for live screening
  • A stable internet connection
  • No prior experience with AI freelance platforms required

Typical Timeline

The steps happen sequentially rather than all at once — contact and explanation typically happen first and can be as quick as a single conversation, followed by verification and screening, then agreement review and signing. There's no fixed total duration since it depends on your own pace through each step.

What Determines Your Terms

Starkworth doesn't use fixed public pricing tiers — every Account Owner and Annotator works under an individually signed agreement. The figures below explain what shapes those terms, not a price list.

Your specific profit-sharing percentage and payout schedule are set out in the agreement you sign in step five — not before. See Understanding Your Profit-Sharing Percentage for how that figure is determined.

Ongoing Support

Starkworth's team manages your account professionally once live, so you sit back rather than handling daily operations yourself. If questions come up after you're live, Support and Contact remain available the same way they were during onboarding.

Example Scenario

Illustrative example — not a real customer case study

Someone reaches out via the Contact form on a Monday, has their explanation call the same week, completes ID verification and live screening the following week, and signs their agreement once every question has been answered. Their account goes live shortly after, with their first weekly payout arriving on the next scheduled Wednesday.
