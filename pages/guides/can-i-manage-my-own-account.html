Can I Manage My Own Account Instead of Starkworth?

What the agreement says about independently operating your own account, and when that's allowed.

Introduction

It's a fair question: if you own the account, can you just run it yourself? The agreement addresses this directly in Section 7, and the short answer is: not without Starkworth's prior written consent.

Key Points

  • Section 3 grants Starkworth authority to operate and manage the account for the agreement's duration.
  • Section 7 specifically prohibits interfering with or independently operating the account without prior written consent.
  • This restriction exists because Starkworth's management is the basis of the profit-sharing arrangement itself.
  • If you want more direct involvement, that's a conversation to have via Contact, not something to do unilaterally.
Operating the account outside these terms without consent could be treated as a breach of the agreement.
