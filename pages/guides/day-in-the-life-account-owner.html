A Day in the Life of a Starkworth Account Owner

What being a Starkworth Account Owner actually looks like day to day, once your agreement is signed and your account is live.

Introduction

Once your agreement is signed and your account is live, the day-to-day annotation work is handled by Starkworth and its Annotators — not by you directly. Your ongoing role is closer to that of a client than a worker: reviewing performance, staying reachable for account-related notices, and collecting your weekly payout.

This is deliberately a lighter time commitment than the Annotator role, which is the whole point of the profit-sharing structure.

Key Points

  • Your credentials and account access remain valid and available to Starkworth for management purposes.
  • You're not expected to complete annotation tasks yourself — that's Starkworth's and its Annotators' role.
  • You can request a weekly earnings summary at any time to see how your account performed.
  • You're responsible for keeping your contact and payment details accurate and up to date.
  • You receive your share of Net Earnings every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST).

What You're Expected to Do

Section 7 of the agreement lists your core responsibilities: provide valid account access, don't interfere with or independently operate the account without written consent, notify Starkworth of any credential or status changes, don't share access outside Starkworth, and keep your payment details accurate.

Being an Account Owner is a managed-account relationship with real obligations — not a fully passive, hands-off investment.
