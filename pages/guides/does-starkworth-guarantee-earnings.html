Does Starkworth Guarantee My Earnings? A Straight Answer

A direct, unambiguous answer to whether Starkworth guarantees income for Account Owners or Annotators.

Introduction

No. Starkworth does not guarantee earnings for either role. This guide exists to state that plainly and explain exactly why, since it's one of the most important things to understand before you register or sign anything.

Key Points

  • All earnings are contingent on the volume of work available on third-party AI freelance platforms.
  • Earnings also depend on the successful completion of tasks — nothing is paid for tasks not completed.
  • Starkworth strives to provide consistent weekly earning opportunities, but "strives" is not a guarantee.
  • Testimonials about payout consistency reflect individual experiences, not a promise of future performance.
This is the same position stated in Starkworth's official Legal Disclaimer, published in full on the FAQ page.
