General Provisions: What 'Entire Agreement' Means for You

What Section 20 of the agreement covers — the entire-agreement clause, severability, waiver, and where to send formal notices.

Introduction

Section 20 is the agreement's final, catch-all section. It ties together how the agreement relates to Starkworth's other policies, and covers a few practical mechanics worth knowing.

Key Points

  • Together with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, the agreement forms the entire agreement between the Parties — superseding prior discussions on the same subject.
  • If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest continues in force, modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.
  • A Party's failure to enforce a provision once isn't a waiver of that provision going forward.
  • Formal notices under the agreement go to contact@starkworth.org or your email address on file.
