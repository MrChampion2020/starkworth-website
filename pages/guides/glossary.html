Starkworth Glossary: Key Terms Explained

Clear definitions of the key terms used across Starkworth's guides, FAQ, and agreement documents — Account Owner, Annotator, payout, and more.

Introduction

A few terms come up repeatedly across the site, and it helps to have them defined clearly in one place. This glossary is a good first stop before reaching out to Support, or whenever a term in another guide isn't immediately clear.

Who This Applies To

Anyone reading Starkworth's guides, FAQ, or agreement documents — this is a reference page, not a role-specific guide.

Why It Matters

Keeping these terms straight makes the rest of the site's guides, FAQ, and agreement documents much easier to follow, and prevents the kind of misunderstanding that leads to avoidable mistakes before signing.

What's Involved

  • Account Owner — the verified individual whose profit-sharing agreement governs a managed AI annotation account.
  • Annotator — the registered individual who performs annotation tasks on a managed account and is paid weekly.
  • Profit-Sharing Agreement — the signed legal document defining the exact earnings split and payment schedule for a specific account.
  • Live Screening — a short video verification session required by AI annotation platforms to confirm the account holder's identity.
  • Payout — the weekly payment issued every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST), calculated against an individual signed agreement.
  • Authenticated Dashboard — the logged-in area where sensitive details like full payment information are collected after approval, never on a public form.
  • Registration Review — the 48-hour window Starkworth uses to evaluate new Annotator applications before approval.
