How Starkworth Reviews and Approves New Applicants

What actually happens after you submit an Annotator registration or Account Owner application, from Starkworth's side.

Introduction

Submitting a form is only the first step — both roles go through a review process before you're fully onboarded. The specifics differ by role, but the underlying goal is the same: confirming you are who you say you are, and that the information you've provided is accurate.

Understanding this process helps set realistic expectations for timing and what happens if something doesn't go through immediately.

Key Points

  • Annotator applications are reviewed within 48 hours of submission.
  • Account Owner applications go through identity verification and platform-mandated live screening before the agreement takes effect.
  • Both processes check the accuracy of the information you provide — Section 6 of the agreement makes this an explicit warranty for Account Owners.
  • Approval outcomes are communicated after review; if you're not approved, use Contact for more detail on your specific application.
