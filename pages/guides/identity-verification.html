How Does Identity Verification Work for Account Owners?

How Starkworth verifies Account Owner identity, why it's required, and how it fits alongside live screening and the profit-sharing agreement.

Introduction

Identity verification exists for a simple reason: AI annotation platforms require confirmation that the person operating an account is a real, verified individual, and that requirement carries through to how Starkworth onboards Account Owners. All you need is a valid, unexpired government-issued ID card.

Who This Applies To

Required for every Account Owner. Annotators go through a separate registration and review process rather than this specific verification step.

Why It Matters

Verification is what makes account ownership meaningful and defensible — it's the record that ties a specific, real individual to a specific account and agreement. Without it, there would be no reliable way to confirm who an account actually belongs to.

What's Involved

  • Submitting a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID
  • Confirming your name matches your registration details
  • Pairing verification with a live screening session

Step-by-Step Process

Starkworth guides you through the full account-opening process, so verification isn't something you do alone or blind — it's paired with a live screening session and comes after you've already received a complete explanation of how the model works. See How to Prepare Your ID Documents for practical preparation tips.

What You Need to Get Started

  • A current passport, driver's license, or national ID card
  • A name on the document matching your registration details
  • An undamaged, clearly legible document

Typical Timeline

Verification happens after the explanation step and before live screening, as part of the sequential Account Owner onboarding process.

What Determines Your Terms

Starkworth doesn't use fixed public pricing tiers — every Account Owner and Annotator works under an individually signed agreement. The figures below explain what shapes those terms, not a price list.

Because this step involves sensitive personal documents, Starkworth does not collect payment details — bank numbers, PayPal, wallet addresses — at this stage or anywhere on a public form. Those are only ever added after approval, inside an authenticated dashboard.

Ongoing Support

If your ID doesn't match a standard format (name changes, non-Latin scripts, expired renewal in progress), raise it with Support before submitting rather than after a delay.

Example Scenario

Illustrative example — not a real customer case study

An Account Owner submits their passport for verification. Their name on the document matches their registration exactly, the photo and text are clear, and verification completes without issue, letting them move on to scheduling live screening.
