What Happens If Your Annotator Account Goes Quiet

What inactivity means for your Annotator account status, and how to pick things back up.

Introduction

Life happens, and you might go a while without completing tasks. Starkworth's site doesn't publish a specific inactivity policy for Annotator accounts the way it does for Account Owner agreements (which lapse after 30 days without active tasks), so here's what's known and what to check directly.

Key Points

  • Your Annotator Dashboard remains accessible with your login even during a quiet period.
  • Pay for a given week reflects only that week's task performance and input — no activity means no payout that week.
  • There's no published automatic account closure policy for Annotators specifically.
  • If you're unsure of your current status, check the "Status" field on your dashboard, or ask via Contact.
For account-specific questions about long-term inactivity, Contact is the direct channel — this isn't detailed publicly.
