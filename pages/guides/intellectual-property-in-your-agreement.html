Understanding Intellectual Property in Your Agreement

What Section 14 of the agreement says about who owns what — your account, your pre-existing IP, and platform-specific IP.

Introduction

Intellectual property clauses can sound intimidating, but Section 14 of the agreement is actually quite short and protective of you: each party keeps its own pre-existing IP, and nothing transfers ownership of your account.

Key Points

  • Each party retains ownership of its own pre-existing intellectual property.
  • Nothing in the agreement transfers ownership of the account itself — that remains yours.
  • Platform-specific intellectual property remains governed by that platform's own separate terms, not Starkworth's agreement.
  • This section works alongside Section 3, which grants operating authority without transferring ownership.
