Is This Legal or Financial Advice? Understanding the Disclaimer

What Starkworth's guides and FAQ content are meant for, and what they explicitly are not.

Introduction

Everything published in Starkworth's Guides library and FAQ, including this page, is for operational purposes — explaining how things work — not for legal, financial, or investment decision-making.

Key Points

  • Guides and FAQ materials are for operational purposes only.
  • None of it constitutes legal, financial, or investment advice.
  • The formal, legally binding documents are the signed profit-sharing agreement and the Terms of Service.
  • You're encouraged to review those binding documents directly before signing, and to seek independent advice if you need it.
If anything in a guide appears to conflict with the actual signed agreement or Terms of Service, the signed legal documents govern.
