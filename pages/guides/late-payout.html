What to Do If Your Payout Is Late

Step-by-step guidance on what to check and who to contact if your Starkworth weekly payout hasn't arrived on schedule.

Introduction

Payouts are processed every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST), so the first thing to check is whether that window has actually passed — some delays are simply timing, especially around bank processing times that vary by country and payment method.

Who This Applies To

Any Account Owner or Annotator who believes their weekly payout hasn't arrived as expected.

Why It Matters

Most "late" payouts turn out to be timing or a details issue rather than an actual error — knowing what to check first saves you from unnecessary worry, and knowing when to escalate gets real issues resolved faster.

What's Involved

  • Confirming the Wednesday 3:00 PM (CST) window has actually passed
  • Checking your payment details on file are current and accurate
  • Contacting Support with your account details if both checks are clear

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Confirm the Wednesday 3:00 PM (CST) payout window has passed.
  2. Check your payment details on file are current and accurate.
  3. Reach out through Support or Contact with your account details.
  4. Keep your own record of expected payout dates and amounts for reference.

What You Need to Get Started

  • Your account details, ready to share with Support
  • A personal record of expected payout dates and amounts based on your agreement

Typical Timeline

Check immediately after the Wednesday 3:00 PM (CST) window if a payout seems missing — the sooner it's raised, the faster it can be checked against your tracked agreement record.

What Determines Your Terms

There's no separate "late payout fee" or penalty structure — the goal is simply to identify and resolve the delay, whether it's a timing issue, outdated details, or a genuine error.

Ongoing Support

After confirming both checks above, reach out through Support or Contact with your account details so the payout can be checked against your signed agreement's records directly — this is far faster than waiting and hoping it resolves on its own.

Example Scenario

Illustrative example — not a real customer case study

An Annotator notices their payout hasn't arrived by Wednesday evening. They check and realize the 3:00 PM CST window was only an hour ago, and their bank's usual processing adds a short delay. By Thursday morning it's arrived. Had it still been missing after 24 hours, the next step would have been contacting Support directly with account details.
