What to Expect During the Live Screening Process

What Starkworth's live screening session involves, why it's required, and how to prepare so it goes smoothly.

Introduction

The live screening is a short session, done over video from your own laptop, and it's a standard requirement of the AI freelance platforms themselves — not something Starkworth adds on top. Its purpose is to confirm that the verified, real person completing onboarding is who they say they are, which protects both you and the integrity of the account.

Who This Applies To

Every Account Owner completes live screening as part of onboarding — it isn't optional and isn't skipped for any account type.

Why It Matters

Screening protects you directly: it confirms that no one else can claim ownership of your account, and it satisfies the underlying platform requirement that keeps the account in good standing. Skipping or faking this step isn't possible, and attempting to work around it would put the account itself at risk.

What's Involved

  • A short video call, done from your own laptop
  • Confirmation of your identity against your submitted ID
  • No technical questions — this is verification, not an interview

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Complete the explanation step so you know what to expect first.
  2. Submit your ID for verification.
  3. Schedule your live screening session.
  4. Join the session with a stable connection and your ID on hand.
  5. Proceed to agreement review and signing once screening is complete.

What You Need to Get Started

  • A laptop with a working camera and microphone
  • Good lighting and a quiet, private space
  • A stable internet connection
  • Your physical ID document on hand, not just a photo of it

Typical Timeline

The session itself is short. It's scheduled after ID verification and before agreement signing, as one step within the broader onboarding sequence described in How to Become a Starkworth Account Owner.

What Determines Your Terms

Starkworth doesn't use fixed public pricing tiers — every Account Owner and Annotator works under an individually signed agreement. The figures below explain what shapes those terms, not a price list.

Live screening doesn't have a cost associated with it — it's a required verification step, not a paid or billable service.

Ongoing Support

If anything about the screening process is unclear beforehand, ask during the explanation step, which happens earlier and is specifically designed to answer these questions. You should never feel rushed into screening without having had it explained first.

They're told to have their government ID ready and to test their camera and microphone beforehand. On the day, the session takes a few minutes, confirms their identity, and they move straight to reviewing their agreement.
