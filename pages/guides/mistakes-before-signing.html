7 Mistakes to Avoid Before Signing Your Agreement

The seven most common mistakes people make before signing a Starkworth agreement, and how to avoid each one.

Introduction

Before you sign anything with Starkworth, avoid these common mistakes. Each one is easy to prevent, but hard to fix retroactively once you've already signed and started working under the agreement.

Who This Applies To

Both prospective Account Owners and Annotators — the specific documents differ, but the discipline of reading carefully before agreeing applies equally to both.

Why It Matters

Every mistake on this list is preventable with a few extra minutes of care. Taking the time to get each one right before signing saves far more time than it costs.

What's Involved

  • Signing without reading the full agreement
  • Not asking questions during the explanation step
  • Providing inaccurate personal or account details
  • Ignoring the payment method notice
  • Assuming verbal promises count
  • Rushing verification or live screening
  • Forgetting to confirm your specific payout schedule and split

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Signing without reading the full agreement — always review every section, since it's legally binding.
  2. Not asking questions during the explanation step, when clarification is easiest to get.
  3. Providing inaccurate personal or account details, which can delay approval or void the agreement later.
  4. Ignoring the payment method notice — you only select a preferred method at registration; full details come later inside your authenticated dashboard.
  5. Assuming verbal promises count — if it isn't in the signed agreement, it isn't binding.
  6. Rushing the live screening or verification without having your documents ready.
  7. Forgetting to confirm the payout schedule and split percentage stated in your specific agreement, since these are individual, not a fixed company-wide number.

What You Need to Get Started

  • Enough time set aside to actually read the full document
  • A written list of questions before your explanation step
  • Accurate personal and account details ready in advance

Typical Timeline

Avoiding these mistakes doesn't add meaningful time to onboarding — if anything, it prevents the delays caused by having to correct errors after the fact.

What Determines Your Terms

Starkworth doesn't use fixed public pricing tiers — every Account Owner and Annotator works under an individually signed agreement. The figures below explain what shapes those terms, not a price list.

None of these seven mistakes relate to cost — they're about accuracy and diligence, which are free but require attention.

Ongoing Support

If you're partway through onboarding and realize you've made one of these mistakes already, contact Support immediately rather than continuing forward and hoping it resolves itself.
