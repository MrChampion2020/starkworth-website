How to Prepare Your ID Documents for Verification

Practical tips for preparing a valid government ID for Starkworth's Account Owner verification, avoiding the most common delays.

Introduction

A little preparation makes identity verification faster and smoother. Use a current, unexpired, government-issued photo ID — a passport, driver's license, or national ID card are the most reliable options. Make sure the document is undamaged and all text is clearly legible; blurry or cropped photos are the most common reason verification takes longer than expected.

Who This Applies To

Every prospective Account Owner, especially anyone who wants to move through onboarding as quickly as possible.

Why It Matters

Most verification delays aren't caused by anything complicated — they're caused by avoidable document-quality issues. A few minutes of preparation now saves days of back-and-forth later.

What's Involved

  • Choosing the right document (passport, driver's license, or national ID)
  • Checking the name matches your registration exactly
  • Confirming the document is undamaged and unexpired
  • Preparing your environment for the live screening call

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Choose a current, unexpired, government-issued photo ID.
  2. Check all four corners and text are clearly legible.
  3. Confirm the name matches what you used to register.
  4. Have the physical document on hand for live screening, not just a photo.
  5. Find a well-lit, quiet space with a stable connection for the call.

What You Need to Get Started

  • Passport, driver's license, or national ID card
  • Good lighting for photographing or displaying the document
  • A laptop with a working camera for the paired live screening session

Typical Timeline

Preparation itself takes minutes, but getting it right the first time is what keeps the overall verification and screening timeline short — see How Does Identity Verification Work for how this step fits into the wider process.

Ongoing Support
