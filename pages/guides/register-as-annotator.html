How to Register as a Starkworth Annotator: Step-by-Step Guide

The complete process for registering as a Starkworth Annotator, from the registration form through approval and your first payout.

Introduction

Registering as an Annotator starts with the registration form, where you provide your name, email, phone number, country of residence, and some background on any prior data-annotation platform experience. You'll also select your preferred payment method, but you won't be asked to enter actual account numbers, PayPal emails, or wallet addresses at this stage.

Who This Applies To

Anyone who wants to earn weekly pay by performing annotation tasks on Starkworth-managed accounts.

Why It Matters

A complete, accurate registration is the fastest path to approval. Incomplete or inaccurate details are one of the most common reasons applications take longer than the standard 48-hour review window.

What's Involved

  • Personal details: name, email, phone number, country of residence
  • Background: any prior AI data-annotation platform experience
  • Preferred payment method (category only, not full account details)
  • Acknowledgement of the profit-sharing terms

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Fill in the registration form with accurate personal details.
  2. Select your preferred payment method (bank transfer, PayPal, or other).
  3. Tick the acknowledgement checkbox confirming you understand the profit-sharing terms.
  4. Submit — Starkworth reviews applications within 48 hours.
  5. Once approved, you're linked to an account and can begin work.

What You Need to Get Started

  • A valid email address and phone number
  • Your country of residence
  • A decision on your preferred payment method category
  • No full payment details required at this stage — those come later, post-approval

Typical Timeline

Starkworth reviews new Annotator applications within 48 hours. After approval, you're linked to an account and weekly payouts follow the standard Wednesday 3:00 PM (CST) schedule from your first working week.

What Determines Your Terms

Starkworth doesn't use fixed public pricing tiers — every Account Owner and Annotator works under an individually signed agreement. The figures below explain what shapes those terms, not a price list.

Annotator pay is based on task performance and input on the account, not a fixed hourly or per-task rate advertised publicly. See How Are Weekly Payouts Calculated? for the full explanation.

Ongoing Support

If you haven't heard back after 48 hours, or want to check on a pending application, reach out through Contact or the Support chat for a status update.

Example Scenario

Illustrative example — not a real customer case study

Someone completes the registration form on a Tuesday, selects PayPal as their preferred payment method, and ticks the acknowledgement checkbox. They hear back Thursday with approval, are linked to an account that week, and receive their first weekly payout the following Wednesday.
