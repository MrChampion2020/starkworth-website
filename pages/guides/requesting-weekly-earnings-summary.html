How to Request and Read Your Weekly Earnings Summary

How Account Owners can request a weekly earnings summary, and how to read the Gross and Net Earnings figures in it.

Introduction

Section 8 of the agreement commits Starkworth to providing a weekly earnings summary "upon reasonable request" — meaning it isn't pushed to you automatically by default, but it is something you're entitled to ask for.

Key Points

  • Request your summary through the Contact page or your account contact channel.
  • A summary should let you see how that week's Gross Earnings, deductions, and your Net Earnings percentage were applied.
  • Requesting a summary doesn't affect your payout timing — Wednesday payments continue on schedule regardless.
  • Keep a personal record of summaries you receive for your own reference.
