What Starkworth Is Responsible For Under Your Agreement

The specific commitments Section 8 of the agreement makes on Starkworth's side, in return for your account access.

Introduction

The agreement isn't one-sided. Section 8 lists six specific commitments Starkworth makes to you as an Account Owner, balancing the responsibilities you take on in Section 7.

Key Points

  • Manage the account professionally and in good faith at all times.
  • Make every reasonable effort to maximise earnings from the account.
  • Pay the Account Owner Share every Wednesday by 3:00 PM (CST) without undue delay.
  • Provide a weekly earnings summary upon reasonable request.
  • Keep your credentials and personal information strictly confidential, per Sections 12 and 13.
  • Notify you promptly of any issues materially affecting the account or expected earnings.
